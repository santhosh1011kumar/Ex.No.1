### 4.2 Explain the types of Generative AI models

### Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)

GANs use two networks: a generator that creates data and a discriminator that evaluates whether the data looks real. They are commonly used for image generation.

<img width="1536" height="1024" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ae7b27e9-1da4-43d4-ad61-a49df183fe11" />

### Variational Autoencoders (VAEs)

VAEs learn compressed representations of data and use them to generate new examples similar to the training data.

<img width="5000" height="2812" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ec7d50a3-c50f-4d06-8a16-323c01f5d8c7" />

### Diffusion Models

Diffusion models learn to generate data by gradually removing noise. They are widely used in AI image-generation systems.

### 4.3 Introduce Large Language Models (LLMs)

<img width="800" height="533" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b14bf7a8-0fa9-4166-b0e6-e33318204ac4" />


Large Language Models are AI models trained on large amounts of text and other suitable data to understand and generate language. They can answer questions, summarize documents, translate languages, and generate code.

### 4.4 Explain the architecture of LLMs

<img width="1280" height="720" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a12dd0d4-ae6a-4c15-a4c3-3caca84c9cf4" />


The Transformer architecture is important in modern language processing. It uses an attention mechanism to identify relationships between tokens in a sequence.
- GPT: Typically uses a decoder-only Transformer to generate text.
- BERT: Uses an encoder-based Transformer to learn contextual representations, especially for language understanding tasks.
- Attention: Helps a model determine which parts of the input are most relevant to a particular token.
